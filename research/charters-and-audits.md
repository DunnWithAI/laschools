# Louisiana charter school finances: where the data is

Researched 2026-11-19. "Verified" = I fetched the source; "UNVERIFIED" = inferred or from a search snippet only. Absence of a link in my sampling is not proof the school posts nothing: several school sites render menus with JavaScript and I only grepped static HTML.

## 1. What the law requires (verified from legis.la.gov text of R.S. 17:3996 unless noted)

- **Audits.** R.S. 17:3996(F): every charter school is subject to "appropriate financial audits in accordance with R.S. 24:513 et seq." (LLA law). R.S. 24:514 requires annual financial reports to LLA; the LLA Governmental Audit Guide (300-1040) says charters may report on the nonprofit model (FASB, with functional expenses) or the governmental model, and LLA accepts either.
- **Budgets.** R.S. 17:3996(G): all charters "shall comply with R.S. 39:1301 through 1315" (Local Government Budget Act). Type 1, 3, 3B, 4 submit budget to the authorizing local board, which sends it to the state superintendent under R.S. 17:88; Type 2 and 5 submit directly to the state superintendent. 17:3996(H): BESE prescribes budgeting/accounting/reporting forms for Type 2 and 5. Per BESE rules (LAC 28:CXXXIX ch. 25, via search snippet, UNVERIFIED current text): July 1-June 30 fiscal year, annual budget due July 31, quarterly reports, Annual Financial Report (AFR) due Sept 30 through LDOE's LEADS portal.
  - So the answer to "must charters adopt/publish budgets?": they must adopt and file budgets under the Local Government Budget Act and with LDOE/authorizer; the LGBA public-inspection and hearing steps apply (LLA school-board checklist: proposed budget available 15 days before adoption, notice of hearing; UNVERIFIED whether LLA treats the charter version identically).
- **Open meetings and public records.** R.S. 17:3996(B)(9)-(10) lists open meetings (R.S. 42:11) and public records (R.S. 44:1) among the laws that DO apply to charters (verified in text). Budgets, contracts, and salary records are requestable.
- **Online posting (Act 370 of 2023, R.S. 17:88.1).** (from the statute text via search; UNVERIFIED lettering) Each public school governing authority, charters included, must post its most recent budget and general summary on its website by Sept 30 each year, post its latest audit within 30 days of LLA acceptance, and, starting FY beginning 7/1/2024, post semiannual actual revenue/expenditure/contract reports. The Treasurer hosts the data; the state uses "Budget Form A" (annual and semiannual Excel templates). The statute makes some provisions conditional on a specific appropriation (not confirmed met).
- **State portal.** Louisiana Treasurer's School Transparency Project (OpenGov): `https://stories.opengov.com/latreasurer/0d98c2db-0666-4632-8979-e152941123f6/published/FmFdbpG9y` (returns 200; announced ~April 2, 2025; reported to cover budgets, salaries, contracts, performance scores for public and charter schools). I did not explore its contents or whether it has bulk download (UNVERIFIED). Treasury page `treasury.la.gov/school-transparency-project` returned 403 to curl/WebFetch.

## 2. Where charter financial data lives (ranked by usefulness)

1. **LLA audit database** (annual audited statements, per entity, back to ~2005+). Best systematic source; see `lla-howto.md`. 142 agency names start `"<Parish> Parish Charter School - "`.
2. **Treasury School Transparency Project / Act 370 Form A** (budget and semiannual actuals, since FY25). Likely the best machine-readable budget source; needs exploration.
3. **Each operator's website.** Required by Act 370 to post budget/audit. Compliance is uneven (see table).
4. **LDOE/BESE.** AFR and budget submissions via LEADS for Type 2/5; LDOE publishes annual charter reports (e.g. `louisianabelieves.com/docs/default-source/school-choice/2015-2016-charter-annual-report.pdf` appeared in search; recent editions UNVERIFIED) and a charter resource page (`doe.louisiana.gov/topic-pages/louisiana-school-choice/charter-schools/charter-schools-resources`, not opened). A 2023-24 AFR highlights PDF is at `leads13.doe.louisiana.gov/lug/afr/Docs for Webpage 2023/...` (not opened).
5. **OPSB / NOLA Public Schools** (authorizer for most New Orleans charters): oversight framework (HB 22 policy) requires charters to meet financial performance expectations; charters submit "NOLA-PS Budget Submission Form A" (seen on the Morris Jeff site). NOLA-PS and New Schools for New Orleans launched a data dashboard (data.nolapublicschools.com) and "School Sustainability Analysis" in Feb 2025 (from a search result; covers finances? UNVERIFIED). OPSB's own audit (FY2025) is at LLA but shows charter flow-through only at the OPSB level.
6. **ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer** for 990s (charters are 501(c)(3) nonprofits; years below are tax-period years found via the ProPublica API; "latest" depends on filing lag). Note that 990s file by EIN and often group multiple schools.
7. **Federal Audit Clearinghouse** single audits (UNVERIFIED coverage, but FirstLine appears).

## 4. Observations for the project

- A charter is an LLA "agency" with its own audited statements; for New Orleans, adding up OPSB + charters is needed to see all public school spending (OPSB's audit shows only its own funds; its pass-through to charters is not charter spending detail).
- Audit format is inconsistent: nonprofit-model (functional expenses: program vs. support, no budget comparison) vs. governmental-model. Do not expect uniform function categories across charters; the state's Form A (Act 370) and LDOE AFR may be the better uniform source for budgets and function-level spending.
- Every audit sampled contained a schedule of compensation to the agency head (CEO or superintendent) with component breakdown. That is a cheap, reliable extraction target.
- Charter operators are mostly multi-school nonprofits; their audits often include a by-school combining statement (KIPP Schedule 4), which gives per-school figures when present.
- Followups: explore the Treasury OpenGov portal for downloadable data; get the LDOE AFR data (LEADS) and charter annual reports; resolve LAVCA, Connections, Einstein, and ReNEW Moton LLA names; fetch the LLA annual Charter School Reports for 2021-2023; check each operator's Act 370 posting (budget by Sept 30; audit within 30 days of LLA acceptance) with a real browser since many sites need JavaScript.
