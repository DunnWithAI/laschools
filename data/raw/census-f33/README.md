# Census Annual Survey of School System Finances (F-33)

Per-district files, fiscal years 1992-2024 (FY1999 not posted at the usual path).
URL pattern: https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/school-finances/tables/<FY>/secondary-education-finance/elsec<YY>.txt
FY2022: the .txt is truncated on the Census site (1,729 rows); use elsec22.xlsx.
Codebook: school24doc.docx. Money in $1,000s. Louisiana = NCESID starting "22".

Scope: 69 parish/city systems + Recovery School District. Charter schools run by
nongovernmental operators are OUT of scope (see codebook, "Charter Schools"); district
payments to them are counted inside the paying district. So Orleans looks tiny.
